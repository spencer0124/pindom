PINDOM unlocks the camera and ticket issuing only inside a 50 m radius of a filming location.

Set the device location to Jumunjin Breakwater: 37.8796220881, 128.8335906768 (use exactly; outside 50 m, verification is refused by design).

1. Sign in with the account above.
2. Map tab, select 주문진 방파제.
3. Tap GPS 인증하기 to see the distance to the radius.
4. Verify inside the radius: the camera opens and a ticket is issued.

Radius, speed and mock-location checks all run server-side.
